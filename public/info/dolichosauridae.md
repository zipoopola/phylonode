The Mosasaur placement is somewhat debated today. Placement within Toxicofera is agreed, and the more common placement is as shown here. The three most common theories are:
1 - Sister to Varanoidea (incl. Monitor lizards) 
2 - Sister to Snakes
3 - Parent to Snakes
The final option is interesting, and has Snakes as decendants from Dolichosauridae. This makes sense for some reasons, as Dolichosauridae also have heavily reduced limbs, and some evidence exists that snakes originated in the water. Otherwise it is one of many examples of convergent limb reduction in the lizard family.
